# Code of Conduct

Algorithm Atlas welcomes contributors and learners with different backgrounds, experience levels, and perspectives. We expect respectful, constructive participation in project discussions, code review, issues, and related community spaces.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and evidence with patience; explain technical criticism clearly.
- Respect differences in experience, language, identity, and learning pace.
- Credit other people's work and correct mistakes without personal attacks.
- Respect privacy and maintainers' decisions about scope and moderation.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, personal insults, deliberate disruption, and disclosure of another person's private information are unacceptable. Do not use project channels to spam or intimidate others.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report concerns privately to maintainer **MD.Khalid Hasan** at [mdkhalidhasan7343@gmail.com](mailto:mdkhalidhasan7343@gmail.com). Share the context needed to understand the incident and avoid unnecessary private information. Reports will be handled with care; confidentiality will be respected to the extent practical without promising absolute secrecy.

The maintainer may ask for corrections, remove inappropriate content, limit participation, or exclude participants in proportion to the behavior. Affected participants may request reconsideration through the same contact. Apply these standards fairly and focus on keeping the project safe and useful for learning.
