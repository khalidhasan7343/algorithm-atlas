# B12 mathematical,score and numerical contracts

Owner MD.Khalid Hasan;MIT. Exactly three canonicals; no external estimator delegates. Common validation/distance computations reuse approved B4/B2 unchanged. RBF computation reuses the approved B1 SVR blocked direct-difference helper; neither signed-zero-sum SVR projection nor unconstrained B10 optimization is suitable for the one-class dual.

## Inputs,ownership and bounds

Training feature matrices:1..256 rows (LOF requires>=2),1..32 features,finite real float64 with magnitude<=1e12. No bool/string/complex/object,NaN imputation,implicit scaling or dimensional flattening. IF/SVM query matrices have0..1024 rows and the same feature dimension;empty query results are empty. Owned input/returned-array copies;failed fits preserve prior state. Public attribute tampering is outside the contract. Local seeded generators do not mutate global RNG. No downloads,unsafe serialization or heavyweight dependencies.

## Scores,thresholds and evaluation

predict labels:+1 inlier,-1 outlier. A decision score>=0 is an inlier,including exact boundary ties. anomaly_scores are oriented high=more anomalous. IF raw score is the positive paper score;score_samples is its negative. OCSVM score_samples is g=Kalpha,anomaly_scores=-g,threshold=-rho,decision=g-rho. LOF raw score is its density ratio (possibly controlled+infinity);score_samples is negative LOF and only scores training samples.

IF/LOF contamination is auto or a finite fraction in(0,.5]. Auto thresholds are respectively.5 and1.5. Numeric contamination uses training raw scores and the linear quantile at1-contamination with position(n-1)*q. Exact quantile endpoints are preserved;positive interpolation weight on an infinite upper endpoint gives+infinity. Strictly greater raw scores are outliers. Thus ties can yield fewer or zero anomalies rather than forced quotas. For threshold+infinity,finite scores have decision+infinity and infinite-score ties have decision0;for finite threshold and infinite raw score,decision=-infinity. This avoids undefined infinity-infinity without fabricating finite raw scores. Contamination is a cutoff assumption,not the true anomaly rate. SVM nu is a model parameter,not a separate contamination quantile.

Evaluation uses -1 as the positive anomaly class. Confusion rows/columns are inlier then anomaly. Precision/recall returnNone when their respective denominators are zero;F1 uses2TP/(2TP+FP+FN) orNone if that denominator is zero. ROC-AUC uses high anomaly scores with half credit for ties and returnsNone for a single class. Average precision integrates precision over tied-score recall jumps,not trapezoidal PR-AUC;no-positive sets returnNone. Extended positive-infinite scores can be ranked explicitly;NaN/negative infinity are rejected for these evaluation inputs. Supervised labels never enter unsupervised fitting.

## Isolation Forest

n_estimators1..128,max_samples auto=min(n,256) or explicit integer1..n,max_depthNone(defaultceil(log2 psi)) or0..32. Each tree draws psi observations without replacement,then chooses uniformly among currently nonconstant features and uniformly splits that feature range. Endpoint-rounding repair to the next float above the minimum preserves two nonempty branches;split routing is left for x<cut,right otherwise. Stop on depth limit,one sample or all coordinates constant. No bootstrap/feature-subset option is claimed. Frozen node diagnostics retain sample counts and depths.

Corrected path h=edges traversed+c(leaf_count). c(0)=c(1)=0,c(2)=1,c(n)=2*sum(1/j,j=1..n-1)-2*(n-1)/n. Harmonic sums are exact finite sums,not log approximations. Paper raw score s=2**(-mean(h)/c(psi)). For psi1,the normalization is degenerate and all supported query scores are defined neutral.5. Tree construction is randomized isolation,not supervised CART. Exact random streams need not match scikit-learn;normalization/constant-feature conventions differ and comparisons must state this.

## One-Class SVM

RBF-only kernel exp(-gamma*||x-z||²),gammaNone=1/d or explicit1e-12..1e12. nu supported domain1e-4..1. Dual objective min.5*alpha.T*K*alpha subjectsum(alpha)=1,0<=alpha<=C=1/(nu*n). Uniform initialization is feasible. The capped-simplex initialization is the exact/tolerance-controlled Euclidean projection of zero onto the feasible set;public projection inputs have magnitude<=100 and cap>=1/n. A96-step monotone shift root computes clip(v-tau,0,C),never clip-and-renormalize. Effective projection cap min(C,1) is redundant by unit mass;KKT/primal diagnostics use originalC. Solver updates are feasible pair-coordinate transfers:increase a coefficient with minimum gradient among non-capped entries,decrease one with maximum gradient among positive entries,and exactly minimize that bounded line using curvature Kii+Kjj-2Kij. Preserve pair mass,check feasibility each step,recompute gradients periodically and certify from a fresh gradient. Stop only when pairwise KKT gap and rho residual<=tolerance;max_iter1..20000 and n*max_iter<=3e6. Report actual objective/primal/dual/gap,not exact optimality. Unconstrained B10 L-BFGS is not substituted. Decision differences within32*eps*(1+abs(g)+abs(rho)) are snapped to zero for roundoff only. KKT gradientg=Kalpha:zero-bound coefficients require g>=rho,upper-bound requireg<=rho,free coefficients g=rho. With no free vector,use justified bounds[max(g at upper),min(g at zero)]. At nu1 all coefficients are fixed1/n;choose rho=max(g),the finite valid endpoint of its half-infinite KKT interval. This endpoint may classify many training samples as outliers. Compare sklearn coefficients/offsets/scores after dividing its LIBSVM scaling bynu*n;do not require unique support sets or nu1 parity.

## Tie-inclusive Local Outlier Factor

Euclidean metric;training-only fit/anomaly_scores()/decision_function()/predict(). No unseen-query novelty API is claimed. k1..n-1. Exclude only the same row index;include every neighbor with distance<=the kth other-row distance,including all ties and duplicate rows. Reachability uses the neighbor's k-distance:reach(p,o)=max(kdist(o),distance(p,o));mean reachr_p defines lrd=1/r_p and LOF=mean(r_p/r_o).

For positive means,compute scaled log-mean reachability and log density ratios to preserve tiny-scale behavior instead of forming overflow-prone lrd quotients. Distinguish exact zero reachability from a positive mean that underflows in a displayed ordinary diagnostic. The original paper assumes distinct points;our declared duplicate extension is the uniform r->r+epsilon limit as epsilon decreases to0. Zero-reach duplicate piles get LOF1;positive reach with any zero-reach neighbor gives genuine LOF+infinity. Log diagnostics distinguish representation overflow from true structural zeros. Finite but unrepresentable LOF raises NumericalError rather than inventing extreme finite values. Detailed density/oracle tests accompany Batch3. sklearn uses fixed-k neighborhoods and an additive1e-10 reachability stabilizer;only compare on matching unique-k-distance,ordinary-scale cases.
